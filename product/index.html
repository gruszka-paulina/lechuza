NAVIO Color 20 slate

The space-saving planter for your windowsill

Item No. 19431

NEW

With the NAVIO planter, you can easily add greenery and life to narrow window sills, sideboards, or garden tables. The elegant oval-shaped planter with a silk-matt finish blends harmoniously into your home and can be perfectly combined with other planters from the Color series. Thanks to the integrated LECHUZA irrigation system, your plants will enjoy optimal care with minimal effort. NAVIO is weatherproof, shatterproof, and fully recyclable. The planter can be used both indoors and outdoors. Excess rainwater and irrigation water can drain away by removing the drain plug on the bottom of the planter.
